About this data
  • Federal campgrounds (Army Corps, Forest Service, National Park Service, BLM, Reclamation, Fish & Wildlife): every campground in the federal recreation system, from Campsite Atlas open data, which is cleaned from the Recreation.gov RIDB export (CC0). Snapshot from September 2026. Phone numbers and current conditions are on each campground's Recreation.gov page.
  • National parks: all 63, each linked to its official nps.gov home and contact pages.
  • State parks: the official park agency for all 50 states. Each one links to that state's full list of parks. Individual state parks aren't bundled yet; add the ones you use or import a list.
  • Scouting America: the four national high adventure bases and the national office. Local council camps aren't bundled; add yours or import your council's list. Find any council at beascout.scouting.org.
  • Your data (added entries, edits, notes, and stars) stays in this browser. Use Export → Backup to keep a copy or move it to another device, and Import to load it back.

Contact details change. Confirm hours, fees, and phone numbers with the managing office before a trip.

Import

Load a backup file exported from this page, or a CSV spreadsheet of camps and parks, such as your council's camp list or a state's parks. Imported rows are added to My entries.

CSV columns

The first row must be column names. Only name is required; other columns are optional and can be in any order:

name, category, type, organization, unit, address, city, state, zip, contact, phone, email, website, reservations, lat, lon, description, notes

category can be scouting, national park, state park, army corps, or public lands. state can be a two-letter code or a full name. Export → Blank import template gives you a ready-made file.
